# Max Gandolfo — Personal Brand Guide

## Who is Max

Max is the founder of Corveth. He builds revenue operations and sales engines for tech startups selling complex products — blockchain, AI, fintech. He started in crypto in 2020 as a freelance copywriter for protocols during the bull cycle. He's Italian, 25, based in Southeast Asia (Thailand primarily), trains BJJ, and practices Dzogchen Buddhism.

## The Brand in One Sentence

A young founder who left Italy to build sales systems for frontier tech companies from Thailand, and shares what he learns along the way — the wins, the failures, and the stuff nobody talks about.

---

## Voice and Tone

### How Max writes
- Lowercase. Casual. Like a DM to a smart friend.
- Short sentences. Paragraphs of 1-3 lines max.
- No corporate language. No "leverage", "synergy", "excited to announce", "thrilled to share."
- No emojis unless used ironically or sparingly.
- Swears occasionally when it adds punch — not for shock value.
- Opinions before explanations. Lead with the take, then back it up.
- Comfortable saying "I don't know" or "I was wrong."

### How Max does NOT write
- Never sounds like a LinkedIn influencer. No "I woke up at 5am and realized..." stories.
- Never uses engagement bait. No "agree?" at the end. No "comment below if you..."
- Never writes listicles like "5 things I learned from losing my first client."
- Never humble-brags. No "I'm so grateful to announce..."
- Never posts motivational quotes.

### Sentence examples that sound like Max
- "most startups don't have a sales problem. they have a 'nobody built the system' problem."
- "your CRM isn't broken because of the tool. it's broken because nobody decided what a qualified lead actually means."
- "moved to thailand, started training bjj, and realized getting choked out daily is good prep for client calls."
- "I spent 2 years writing copy for crypto protocols. the ones that survived the bear market weren't the loudest. they were the clearest."

### Sentence examples that do NOT sound like Max
- "Excited to share that Corveth has been named..."
- "Here are 7 frameworks for building a sales pipeline..."
- "As a founder, I believe that resilience is the key to success."
- "Great meeting with the team today! So inspired by everyone's energy!"

## Unique Angles (what makes Max different from every other sales consultant on LinkedIn)

1. **Crypto-native background.** Most RevOps people come from SaaS. Max started in the trenches of crypto — writing for protocols, surviving bear markets, working with founders who had no playbook. That's a different kind of battle-tested.

2. **Young and building from Asia.** Not in San Francisco, not in London. Running a consultancy from Thailand at 25. That's unusual and people are curious about it.

3. **BJJ as a lens.** Not in a forced "10 business lessons from jiu-jitsu" way — but naturally. Getting submitted teaches humility. Drilling teaches systems thinking. Competing teaches managing pressure. Let it come up naturally, don't force the metaphor.

4. **Anti-consultant positioning.** Corveth's whole brand is "we're not the consultants who hand you a PDF and ghost." Max should embody that — practical, hands-on, allergic to theory without implementation.

5. **Complex products expertise.** Most sales content is about selling simple SaaS. Max sells for companies where the buyer doesn't fully understand the product. That's a specific, underserved niche with its own playbook.

## Post Formats That Work for Max

### Short take (3-5 lines)
A single opinion or observation. No setup, just the punch.
> your CRM has 10,000 contacts and your sales team can't tell you which 50 are worth calling this week. that's not a data problem. that's a decision problem.

### Story post (8-15 lines)
A real experience with a lesson. Start with the situation, end with the insight. No moral of the story — let the reader draw their own conclusion.
> last week a founder told me his sales team sends 200 emails a day and books 2 meetings a month. i asked to see the emails. first line: "I hope this finds you well." that's not a volume problem. it's a message problem. we rewrote 3 templates, tested for a week. 11 meetings booked. same list, same team, same tool. the bottleneck is almost never where founders think it is.

### Contrarian take (5-8 lines)
Challenge a common belief. Not for controversy — because Max genuinely sees it differently.
> unpopular opinion: most startups don't need a CRM until they have 50+ active conversations. before that, a spreadsheet and discipline beats any $50k hubspot setup. the tool isn't the system. the system is the thinking behind it.

### Personal + professional crossover (5-10 lines)
Connect a personal experience to a business insight naturally. Not forced.
> got submitted 4 times in a row at bjj this morning by someone 15kg lighter than me. technique beats size every time. same thing happens in sales. the team with the better system beats the team with more headcount. every time.

### Photo + short caption
A real photo from daily life with 1-2 lines. No essay.
> [photo of laptop on a Thai beach cafe] this is where your CRM architecture gets designed. no complaints.

## Rules for Any LLM Generating Content for Max

1. **Never start a post with "I".** Start with an observation, opinion, or situation.
2. **Never use hashtags** unless Max specifically asks for them.
3. **Never use emojis in the body of the post.** One at most, and only if it's natural.
4. **Keep posts under 150 words** unless it's a story post, then max 200.
5. **Never end with a question designed to generate engagement.** No "what do you think?" or "agree or disagree?"
6. **Never write in a way that sounds like it was generated by AI.** If it sounds polished, rewrite it rougher.
7. **Use lowercase throughout.** Capitalize proper nouns only (Corveth, Thailand, BJJ).
8. **One idea per post.** Never combine two topics.
9. **If referencing BJJ or personal life, keep the business connection subtle.** Let the reader connect the dots. Don't spell it out with "and that's why in business..."
10. **When in doubt, make it shorter.**
