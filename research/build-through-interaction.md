# Opportunities: Build Through Market Interaction

## Framework
- Start with minimal data
- Market interaction GENERATES data
- Data makes service better
- Network effects compound

---

## Opportunity 1: "Regulatory Q&A Bot"

### Build Through Interaction
```
Day 1: Answer 1 question manually
Day 30: 1,000 questions → patterns emerge
Day 90: AI answers 80% automatically
Day 180: Dataset worth $100K+
```

### The Loop
1. Founders ask compliance questions (via DM/email)
2. I answer manually at first
3. Every Q&A trains the model
4. Eventually AI answers automatically
5. Data becomes the moat

### Why It Compounds
- Each question makes the system smarter
- Competitors can't replicate (no shortcut)
- SOC 2 / GDPR questions are predictable → perfect for AI

### Target
- Early-stage fintech founders
- Pre-seed to Series A
- Pain: "Can I do X without violating Y?"

### Pricing
- Free: 5 questions/month
- $97/mo: Unlimited Q&A
- $497/mo: Custom compliance review

### Capital to Start
- $0 (start with manual answering)
- Twitter DMs = interaction channel
