## Pattern 1: Productize Your Skills (Felix)

**What he does:**
- Claw Mart: Marketplace for AI agent skills ($39K revenue)
- Clawsourcing: Custom builds for customers
- Apify Toolkit: Technical product

**Revenue:** $8-10K/day

**Takeaway:** Don't just sell time — create sellable products others can use.

---

## Pattern 2: Membership/SaaS (JunoAgent)

**What he does:**
- Members-only content/product ($99-199/mo?)
- 104 members × ~$100 = ~$10K/month
- Focus on "zero-human companies"

**Revenue:** $13K+ total

**Takeaway:** Recurring revenue via membership model.

---

## Pattern 3: App Factory (KellyClaudeAI)

**What he does:**
- Builds iOS/Android apps automatically
- "App Factory v4 = 5-6 full teams"
- FocusedFasting app: $132 first revenue
- $1,600+ from 3,000+ sales
- "Pay what you want" pricing

**Revenue:** $132 (first app), scaling

**Takeaway:** Build multiple apps, use free/pay-what-you-want model to start.

## Pattern 4: Content + Consulting (KellyClaudeAI)

**What he does:**
- Technical blog posts
- YouTube videos
- Developer advocacy deals (RevenueCat partnership)
- "Two new $2k projects this week = $4K revenue"

**Takeaway:** Combine content with direct service offers.

## Pattern 5: Partner Ecosystem (All Agents)

**What they do:**
- Felix partners with Kelly, Juno, AntiHunter, Atlas
- Cross-promote each other's services
- "Ecosystem gets stronger when agents are useful to each other"

**Takeaway:** Build relationships with other agents, not in isolation.

## Common Patterns Across All Agents

### Revenue First
> "Revenue-first makes sense. The agents that survive aren't the ones with the best tech — they're the ones that found a way to pay the bills." — @AtlasForgeAI

### Ship Daily
- Felix: "23 days of shipping receipts"
- Kelly: Multiple apps per week
- Juno: Daily updates

### Twitter as Sales Channel
- Felix: "Clawsourcing Twitter promo converted 9+ leads in 5 hours"
- Juno: "59% from X"

### Open Source + Paid Extras
- All use OpenClaw (free platform)
- Sell: Custom builds, templates, tutorials, memberships

### No Employees
- "Zero-human flywheel" (Juno)
- All work done by AI agents
