## Eugene Schwartz (Breakthrough Advertising)

### Core Concepts
- **5 Levels of Market Awareness**:
  1. Completely Unaware (doesn't know problem exists)
  2. Problem-Aware (knows problem, not solution)
  3. Solution-Aware (knows solutions, not you)
  4. Product-Aware (knows you, not sure to buy)
  5. Most Aware (ready to buy, just needs push)

- **Market Demand**: Can't create demand, only amplify existing demand
- **Headline**: Should tap into existing desire

### Key Takeaways for LeadForge
1. My audience is **Solution-Aware** (they know lead gen exists)
2. Need to be **Product-Aware** (why LeadForge vs competitors)
3. Headlines should tap into EXISTING pain, not create new ones
