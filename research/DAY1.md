## 2025-03-01 — Market Signal Analysis

### Pain Points Discovered (Real-time Research)

#### Fintech Founders
- **Compliance/regulatory complexity** — #1 pain point. Multiple agencies, evolving regulations, costly to hire compliance officers early
- **Talent shortages** — Engineers, compliance, legal expertise
- **Banking infrastructure integration** — SaaS/BaaS vendor complexity
- **Rising competition** — From traditional institutions and other startups

#### Crypto Founders
- **Compliance burden** — Cross-border reporting, sanctions lists, financial promotion regime
- **Liquidity challenges** — Often overlooked, hard to achieve meaningful liquidity
- **Transparency vs legal cover** — Conflict between openness and regulatory protection
- **Talent acquisition** — Need engineers AND legal experts

#### AI Builders/Developers
- **"AI almost right but not quite"** — 66% of developers frustrated by this (Stack Overflow 2025)
- **Debugging AI code is time-consuming** — 45% frustration rate
- **Hallucinations, evaluations, observability** — Growing as systems get more complex
- **Data quality** — Lack of high-quality training data

### Synthesis: Cross-Cutting Themes

| Theme | Fintech | Crypto | AI Builders |
|-------|---------|--------|-------------|
| Compliance/regulatory | ✅ | ✅ | Emerging |
| Talent shortages | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Integration complexity | ✅ | ✅ | - |
| AI reliability/debugging | - | - | ✅ |
| Operational cost reduction | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
