# Prompt: Corveth Consulting Visual Identity

## Context
You are designing the visual brand identity for **Corveth Consulting** based on the completed positioning document.

## Company Background
- **Name:** Corveth Consulting
- **Tagline:** "The Revenue Operations Partner for Companies Ready to Scale"
- **Alternative Tagline:** "We Don't Leave Until It's Working"
- **Industry:** Revenue Operations Consulting
- **Target Audience:** Growth-stage B2B SaaS companies ($3M-$30M ARR)

## Positioning Summary (from Issue #21)
- **Core Promise:** Help growth-stage companies scale revenue ($5M-$50M) without operational chaos
- **Key Differentiation:** Implementation over assessment — "we stay until it works"
- **Personality:** Practical, grounded, action-oriented, no-nonsense
- **Vibe:** Professional but not corporate, experienced but not stuffy, competent but approachable

## Your Task
Design a visual identity system that reflects this positioning. Create a **Brand Guidelines Document** covering:

### 1. Logo Design
- Primary logo concept
- Logo variations (horizontal, icon-only, stacked)
- Clear space and minimum size
- Don'ts (what to avoid)

### 2. Color Palette
- Primary colors (with hex codes)
- Secondary/accent colors
- Background colors
- Color usage guidelines
- Accessibility notes

### 3. Typography
- Primary typeface (headings)
- Secondary typeface (body)
- Font weights and usage
- Size scale

### 4. Visual Style
- Imagery style (photos, illustrations, icons)
- Graphic elements
- Patterns or textures (if any)

### 5. Brand Applications
- Business cards
- Letterhead
- Social media avatars
- Website mood board

## Constraints
- Keep it professional but approachable
- Avoid "big consulting" corporate feel
- Should feel like a growth-stage company: modern, agile, capable
- Practical enough for a solo consultant to maintain

## Output Format
Create a Brand Guidelines Document with:
1. Each section above with specific recommendations
2. Links to suggested fonts, colors (Hex/RGB codes)
3. Simple enough to hand to a designer or use with AI image tools
4. 2-3 mood/inspiration references
