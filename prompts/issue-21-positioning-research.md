# Prompt: Corveth Consulting Positioning Research

## Context
You are helping define the market positioning and brand strategy for **Corveth Consulting**. The goal is to create a clear positioning document that will drive all marketing, outreach, and sales messaging.

## Company Background
- **Name:** Corveth Consulting
- **Current assumption:** Revenue operations consulting, potentially web3-focused
- **Target:** Growth-stage companies
- **Desired outcome:** Help companies scale revenue ($1M → $10M)

## Your Task
Research and deliver a positioning document covering these 5 areas:

### 1. Market & Competitor Research
- Who are the competitors in revenue ops / growth consulting?
- What do they charge? What's their positioning?
- What's missing in the market? What are gaps?

### 2. Target Customer Definition
- Ideal client profile (industry, company size, revenue stage)
- Key pain points you solve
- Budget range (what do similar consultants charge?)
- How do they currently solve this problem?

### 3. Value Proposition
- What exactly do you deliver? (Not vague "revenue ops")
- What is the core offer?
- What are 2-3 specific services or outcomes?

### 4. Differentiation
- Why Corveth? What makes you different?
- What unique experience, methodology, or perspective?
- What claims can you back up?

### 5. Messaging Framework
- Primary headline / tagline
- 2-3 variations for different contexts
- Key talking points for outreach
- How to describe what you do in 1 sentence, 1 paragraph

## Constraints
- Be specific, not generic
- Back claims with reasoning
- If stuck between options, pick one and explain why
- Prioritize action over perfection - done > perfect

## Output Format
Create a **Positioning Document** with:
1. Executive summary (1 paragraph)
2. Each of the 5 sections above
3. Recommended next steps
4. 2-3 specific positioning options to test
