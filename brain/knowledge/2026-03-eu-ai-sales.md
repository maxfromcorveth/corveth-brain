## Key Insight
Three major underserved niches identified. The real gap isn't "AI sales tools" — it's AI sales tools built for Europe's regulatory reality (GDPR, EU AI Act) with explainability baked in.

## Details

### Top 3 Underserved Niches
1. **Compliance Automation** — US tools treat GDPR as afterthought. EU AI Act implementation creating demand.
2. **SMB/Mid-Market** — €8-400M revenue companies ignored. Too big for startups, too small for enterprise.
3. **Real-Time Revenue Intelligence** — Only 7% of sales teams hit 90% forecast accuracy.

### Market Size
- European AI agents: £2B (2025) → £16B (2035)
- 125% CAGR
- €14B European SaaS AI spend expected 2025

### Key Pain Points
- 74% started AI projects, only 14% deployed
- 70% of sales reps don't trust AI without explanations
- Data quality is #1 barrier (60% cite it)

### Pricing Benchmarks
- Growth tier: €80-175/user/month
- Enterprise: €150-550/user/month

## Why It Matters
Product positioning opportunity: Lead with "built for Europe" compliance-first angle. Differentiator is regulatory fit, not AI sophistication.
