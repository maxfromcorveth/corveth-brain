# Research Prompt Template

Use this to structure AI research requests.

```
## Research: [TOPIC]

## What I Need
[1-3 specific questions to answer]

## Context
[Brief background - who cares, why now]

## Sources Preferred
- [e.g., European sources, recent news]
- [e.g., competitor websites]

## Output Format
- Key findings (bullet points)
- Sources (links)
- Next steps (what to do with this)

## Deadline
[When you need it]
```

---

## Example: Used for EU AI Sales Automation

```
## Research: AI Sales Automation Market in Europe

## What I Need
1. Top 3 underserved niches
2. Gaps in current solutions (Clay, Apollo, Cognism)
3. Emerging trends founders want but can't get

## Context
Building Corveth Brain - product for AI agent users.
Need market validation for go-to-market strategy.

## Sources Preferred
- European market reports
- Founder interviews/testimonials
- Competitor pricing

## Output Format
- Findings with sources
- 3 potential customer segments
- 90-day roadmap suggestion

## Deadline
This week
```
