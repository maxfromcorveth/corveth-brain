# Corveth Consulting - Brand Sheet

A quick reference for consistent branding.

---

## Logo

**Primary:** "Corveth" wordmark with gradient C icon

**Icon:** Gradient C (Teal #00A896 → Gold #D4A84B)

**Usage:**
- Use full logo for headers
- Use icon for favicons, social avatars
- Never stretch, rotate, or change colors

## Tone of Voice

**Voice:** Practical, direct, no-nonsense

**Principles:**
- Focus on outcomes, not features
- Say what you mean, no jargon
- Confident but not arrogant

**Avoid:**
- Corporate buzzwords
- Over-promising
- Vague claims
