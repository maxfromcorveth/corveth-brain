# Corveth Daily Outreach SOP

**Purpose:** Daily workflow for LinkedIn outreach

---

## Before You Start

- Open Google Sheet: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1HvdIku0WS5CtUslklZa4EbQm2HNgu-knE9xCknlchA8
- Open LinkedIn in another tab

---

## Step 1: Get Leads

1. Open Google Sheet → Filter by **Stage = "New"**
2. Pick top 10 leads
3. For each lead:
   - Open their LinkedIn profile
   - Check recent posts/news for personalization

---

## Step 2: Send Connection Request

1. Click **Connect** on their profile
2. Paste **First Message** (personalize 1-2 details)
3. Send

---

## Step 3: Update Sheet

After sending:
- Add today's date in **Sent Date** column
- Change **Stage** to "Contacted"

---

## Step 4: Follow-Up (If Any)

Check **Stage = "Follow-up 1 sent"** (from 3-5 days ago)
- If no response → Send Follow-up Message
- Update Stage to "Follow-up 2 sent"

Check **Stage = "Follow-up 2 sent"** (from 10 days ago)
- If no response → Send Breakaway Message
- Update Stage to "Breakaway sent"

---

## Daily Goals

- Send 10-20 first messages
- Follow up on 5-10 leads
- Update sheet after every action

---

## Message Templates

### First Message

Hey [Name],

Saw [recent news/funding/announcement]. Looks like [Company] is [what they're building or achieving].

Quick context: I've been in revenue operations for years. Built marketing for companies that actually scaled. Here's what I know: most founders fly blind because their CRM is a mess and sales + marketing can't agree on anything.

Not pitching. Just curious if any of that resonates. If it does, happy to share what I've seen work.

Max

---

### Follow-up (Day 3-5)

Just bumping this up.

One thing I've noticed with [Company's stage] — the revenue operations piece usually becomes a headache around now. Forecasting gets fuzzy, CRM turns into a data graveyard, and suddenly you can't explain to the board where revenue is coming from.

If any of that sounds familiar, happy to chat. No pitch — just context from someone who's been there.

---

### Breakaway (Day 10)

Last msg from me on this.

If revenue operations ever becomes a headache — forecasting, CRM, alignment between sales and marketing — I'm around. Not a typical consultant who hands you a PDF and vanishes.

Best of luck with [Company].

---

## Tips

- Personalize at least one detail per message
- Don't skip the follow-ups (80% of responses come after first follow-up)
- Track everything in the sheet
- Be consistent — better to do 10/day than 50 one day and none for a week
