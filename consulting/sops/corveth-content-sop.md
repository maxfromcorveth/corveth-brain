---

## Step 1: Get Content

Ask me to generate content:

```
Generate 3 LinkedIn posts for this week about [topic/theme]
```

Or specify:
```
/linkedin-post [topic]
```

I'll generate using your ICP voice and Blair principle.

---

## Step 2: Review & Post

1. Review generated content
2. Edit if needed
3. Post to LinkedIn
4. Note post URL in tracking

---

## Step 3: Track (Simplified)

After posting, just note in sheet:
- Did you get any DMs? (Yes/No)
- Any comments worth following up?

That's it. Don't overcomplicate.
