# ICP Language Research

## Executive Summary

This document captures how SaaS founders actually talk about their revenue, sales, and operations problems. Key finding: founders DON'T use consultant jargon like "revenue leakage" or "RevOps" - they use visceral, emotional language.

---

## Real Founder Language

### The Most Common Phrases

| Founder Says | Consultant Would Say |
|--------------|---------------------|
| "Flying blind" | Lack of visibility |
| "CRM is a mess" | Data quality issues |
| "Deals dying quietly" | Pipeline churn |
| "Two truths, zero alignment" | Sales/Finance misalignment |
| "Revenue dropped 40%" | Sales rep underperformance |
| "Profitless growth trap" | Inefficient scaling |
| "Adjustments before board" | Forecast manipulation |
| "Driving with eyes closed" | No predictive analytics |

## What Founders DON'T Say

Notably ABSENT from founder vocabulary:
- "Revenue leakage" ← consultant term
- "RevOps alignment" ← vendor jargon
- "Pipeline optimization" ← technical term
- "Revenue intelligence" ← vendor-created
- "Go-to-market efficiency" ← business school language

---

## Awareness Levels

### Problem-Aware Only
- "We're flying blind"
- "Revenue dropped 40%"
- Focus: symptoms, not solutions

### Solution-Aware
- "Does revenue forecasting software exist?"
- "Is anyone actually auditing their CRM?"
- Focus: looking for specific answers

### Actively Evaluating
- "Two truths, zero alignment"
- "CRM is a mess because no one owns it"
- Focus: ready to buy

## Copywriting Recommendations

### Headlines That Work
- Use "Flying blind" metaphor
- Direct questions: "Do you know if you'll hit your number?"
- Concrete: "Know Your Numbers"

### Language to Use
| Instead of... | Use... |
|--------------|--------|
| Revenue leakage | Deals dying quietly |
| RevOps alignment | Two truths, zero alignment |
| Pipeline optimization | CRM is a mess |
| Revenue intelligence | I have no idea if we'll hit our number |
| Go-to-market efficiency | Sales process doesn't work |

### Emotional Words from Research
- "Flying blind"
- "Chaos"
- "Bullsh*t"
- "Malpractice"
- "Kills me"
- "Mess"
- "Dying quietly"
