# Corveth Consulting

## Market Positioning & Brand Strategy Document

---

## Executive Summary

Corveth Consulting enters a market ripe for disruption. The revenue operations (RevOps) consulting space has grown exponentially over the past five years, driven by companies recognizing that siloed sales, marketing, and customer success functions are a primary barrier to scalable growth. Yet the market remains fragmented between expensive generalist consultancies that treat RevOps as a sub-practice and boutique firms that lack the strategic depth to drive transformational change.

This positioning document establishes Corveth Consulting's strategic foundation as a specialized revenue operations partner for growth-stage companies. The core thesis: Corveth Consulting exists to help companies scale revenue from $5M to $50M ARR and beyond—without the operational chaos that typically accompanies that growth.

The document identifies a clear market opportunity in the $10K-$75K project range where growth-stage companies need strategic guidance but cannot justify McKinsey-level fees. It establishes differentiated positioning around practical, implementation-ready consulting rather than theoretical frameworks, with optional web3 sector specialization as a potential leverage point. Three distinct positioning options are provided for testing, each targeting a different aspect of the market gap.

## 1. Market & Competitor Research

### 1.1 Competitive Landscape Overview

The revenue operations consulting market divides into three distinct tiers, each with significant positioning gaps that Corveth Consulting can exploit.

**Tier 1: Generalist Consultancies** (McKinsey, Deloitte, BCG, Accenture)

These firms include RevOps as a component within larger digital transformation or sales transformation practices. Their strengths include credibility with boards and investors, global reach, and deep analytical capabilities. However, they carry critical weaknesses for the growth-stage market: average engagement sizes start at $250K+, making them inaccessible to companies under $50M ARR. Their RevOps work often focuses on strategy documents that never translate into implementation. They also tend to deploy junior consultants who lack hands-on operational experience. Companies that engage Tier 1 firms frequently report "beautiful slides, no execution."

**Tier 2: Technology-Focused Consultants** (Salesforce Implementation Partners, HubSpot Agencies)

This tier includes firms whose primary value lies in technology implementation rather than revenue strategy. Examples include firms positioning as "Salesforce Gold Partners" or "HubSpot Elite Agencies." These firms excel at configuring platforms but typically lack the strategic perspective to optimize revenue processes holistically. Their revenue model incentivizes technology adoption over revenue outcomes, creating inherent conflict. Growth-stage companies frequently discover post-implementation that they have expensive technology doing inefficient things.

**Tier 3: Boutique RevOps Consultants and Individual Practitioners**

The market includes hundreds of independent consultants and small boutique firms offering RevOps services. These range from former sales VPs turned consultants to operations leaders who left corporate roles. Pricing varies widely from $150-$400/hour, with project engagements typically between $5K-$30K. This tier offers the best value-to-expertise ratio but suffers from inconsistency in deliverable quality, limited capacity, and often narrow specialization (some only do comp planning, others only do CRM hygiene). Few have built scalable methodologies or brand recognition.

### 1.2 Competitive Positioning Analysis

| Competitor Type | Typical Positioning | Price Point | Key Strength | Key Weakness |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Tier 1 Firms | "Global strategy, enterprise scale" | $250K+ | Credibility, resources | Cost, junior staff, theoretical |
| Tech Partners | "Technology experts, platform implementation" | $50K-$200K | Technical platform knowledge | Strategy-agnostic, vendor bias |
| Boutique Consultants | "Hands-on, experienced operators" | $5K-$30K | Practical expertise, affordability | Limited capacity, inconsistent delivery |
| Corveth Opportunity | "Growth-stage specialists, implementation-ready" | $10K-$75K | Strategic + practical, growth focus | Building brand awareness |

### 1.3 Identified Market Gaps

**The "Middle Market" Gap:** Growth-stage companies ($5M-$50M ARR) represent the largest underserved segment. They have complex enough operations to need strategic help but lack the budget for Tier 1 firms. They need more than technology configuration but less than full-scale transformation. This is precisely where Corveth Consulting should operate.

**The Implementation Gap:** Most RevOps consultants deliver assessments and recommendations. Growth-stage companies need partners who implement alongside them, not just advise from the sidelines. There's a significant market opportunity for consultants who "do the work" rather than just describe it.

**The Web3/Specialization Gap:** While the broader RevOps market is crowded, emerging sectors like web3, crypto, and decentralized finance have unique operational challenges (token-based incentive structures, global distributed teams, regulatory uncertainty) that generalist consultants struggle to address. A focused positioning in this vertical could command premium pricing while facing less competition.

## 2. Target Customer Definition

### 2.1 Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

**Primary Target: Growth-Stage B2B SaaS Companies**

Corveth Consulting's ideal client meets the following criteria:

**Industry Focus:**
- B2B SaaS (primary)
- Enterprise SaaS
- Cloud infrastructure
- FinTech
- Web3/Crypto (secondary vertical for specialized positioning)
- Open to adjacent sectors: professional services, e-commerce platforms, marketplace businesses

**Company Characteristics:**
- Revenue Stage: $3M-$30M ARR (core focus), with ability to serve up to $100M ARR
- Funding Stage: Series A through Series C
- Employee Count: 30-200 employees
- Growth Rate: 40%+ year-over-year growth (or targeting that rate)
- Geographic Focus: US-based (with remote-friendly delivery), potential UK/EU expansion

**Organizational Characteristics:**
- Has established sales and marketing teams but is experiencing coordination failures
- Has implemented (or is implementing) a CRM but isn't getting value from it
- Recently hired or plans to hire a VP of Revenue or similar role
- Has received board pressure to improve revenue predictability
- Is preparing for next funding round and needs operational metrics in order

### 2.2 Key Pain Points

Corveth Consulting solves the following specific problems:

**Pain Point 1: Revenue Leakage from Disconnected Systems**

Growth-stage companies typically have multiple tools that don't communicate—marketing automation, CRM, customer support, billing, and financial systems operate in silos. Data flows break at every handoff, leading to inaccurate forecasting, missed renewals, and poor customer insights. Companies often lose 10-20% of revenue to these disconnections without realizing it.

**Pain Point 2: Sales and Marketing Misalignment**

Marketing generates leads that sales ignores. Sales closes deals that marketing could have nurtured. Neither team agrees on what constitutes a qualified opportunity. This misalignment wastes resources and creates inconsistent customer experiences. In growth-stage companies, this problem compounds as headcount increases faster than process maturity.

**Pain Point 3: Operational Chaos During Scaling**

Companies that grew 100%+ last year often have operational infrastructure built for half their current size. Their compensation plans are outdated. Their territories don't make sense. Their onboarding processes can't keep up. They need to professionalize operations without killing the entrepreneurial energy that drove their early growth.

**Pain Point 4: Lack of Revenue Predictability**

Investors and boards demand predictable revenue. Growth-stage companies struggle to forecast accurately because their data is incomplete, their processes are inconsistent, and they lack the analytical infrastructure to model future performance. This creates tension in fundraising and board meetings.

**Pain Point 5: CRM and Technology Underutilization**

Companies spend significant money on Salesforce, HubSpot, Marketo, and other platforms but use only 20-30% of available functionality. They've customized their CRM to match bad processes rather than optimizing processes first. They need guidance on what's actually valuable versus what looks impressive.

### 2.3 Budget Range

Corveth Consulting should target project engagements in the following ranges:

| Tier | Project Scope | Investment Range | Typical Duration |

The tiered structure allows clients to start small and expand, reducing purchase anxiety while building long-term relationships.

## 3. Value Proposition

### 3.1 What Corveth Consulting Delivers

Corveth Consulting provides implementation-focused revenue operations strategy that helps growth-stage companies build the operational foundation for scalable, predictable revenue growth. Unlike consultants who deliver reports and walk away, Corveth stays engaged until changes are actually working.

**The Core Promise:** Corveth Consulting doesn't just tell you how to scale revenue—we build the operational engine that makes scaling possible.

### 3.2 Core Offer

**Revenue Operations Architecture**

Corveth designs and implements the complete operational infrastructure that growth-stage companies need to scale:
- **Process Design:** End-to-end revenue processes that connect marketing, sales, customer success, and finance
- **Technology Stack:** Right-sized technology recommendations and implementation (not vendor-pushed solutions)
- **Data Infrastructure:** Metrics definitions, dashboards, and reporting that drive decisions
- **Organizational Design:** Role definitions, comp plans, territory models, and team structures
- **Playbook Development:** Documented processes that enable consistent execution as the team grows

### 3.3 Specific Services and Outcomes

**Service 1: Revenue Operations Assessment & Roadmap**

A comprehensive evaluation of current revenue operations maturity with a prioritized implementation roadmap.

*Deliverables:*
- Current state assessment across all revenue functions
- Gap analysis against revenue operations best practices
- Prioritized roadmap with effort/success estimates
- Quick wins identification (typically 3-5 items that can be fixed in 30 days)

*Typical Outcome:* 30-50% improvement in lead-to-opportunity conversion within 90 days; clear visibility into revenue pipeline health

---

Transform the CRM from a data graveyard into a revenue-generating asset.

*Deliverables:*

- User adoption strategy and training materials
- Dashboard and reporting suite

*Typical Outcome:* 40% reduction in admin time; 25% improvement in forecast accuracy; CRM adoption rates above 90%

**Service 3: Sales & Marketing Alignment**

Create seamless handoffs and shared definitions between marketing and sales.

*Deliverables:*
- Service level agreements between marketing and sales

- Nurture and follow-up process documentation
- Attribution model implementation

*Typical Outcome:* 20-30% improvement in marketing-sourced pipeline; 35% reduction in lead response time; agreement on qualified opportunity definitions

## 4. Differentiation

### 4.1 What Makes Corveth Different

**Differentiation 1: Implementation Over Assessment**

The RevOps consulting market is flooded with firms that deliver impressive assessments and then disappear. Corveth differentiates by measuring success on implementation completion, not deliverable delivery. Every engagement includes "done with you" implementation support, not just "done for you" recommendations.

*Reasoning:* Growth-stage companies are tired of paying $30K for a strategy document they don't have time to implement. They want a partner who gets in the trenches and makes things work.

**Differentiation 2: Growth-Stage Specialization**

Corveth focuses exclusively on the inflection point where companies need professional operations but don't yet have the budget for enterprise consultants. This focus creates deep expertise in the specific challenges of this stage—limited resources, competing priorities, the need to move fast, and the importance of not over-engineering solutions.

*Reasoning:* Enterprise consultants apply methodologies designed for large companies. Boutique consultants often lack experience with the complexity of $20M+ ARR companies. Corveth's sweet spot is exactly where most other firms are weakest.

**Differentiation 3: Practical, Not Theoretical**

Corveth's team has actually done the jobs our clients are trying to optimize. We've run sales teams, managed marketing budgets, built compensation plans, and dealt with the day-to-day chaos that growth companies face. We don't propose theoretical ideal states—we build practical solutions that work given real constraints.

*Reasoning:* Many RevOps consultants have never held operational roles. They recommend processes that look good on paper but fail in practice. Corveth's operational background creates credibility and practical solutions.

### 4.2 Unique Experience, Methodology, or Perspective

**The "Revenue Operating System" Methodology**

Corveth brings a unique integrated perspective to revenue operations. Rather than treating RevOps as separate functions that need coordination, Corveth views it as a single system with four interdependent components:

1. **Strategy:** What are we trying to achieve?
2. **Process:** How do we execute consistently?
3. **Technology:** What tools enable our process?
4. **Data:** How do we measure and improve?

This integrated view prevents the common failure mode where companies optimize one component (new CRM) without addressing the others (process, data, skills), leading to expensive implementations that don't deliver results.

**Web3 Sector Expertise (Optional Differentiation)**

If Corveth chooses to specialize in web3/crypto, the unique value proposition includes:
- Experience with token-incentivized growth models
- Understanding of global distributed team compensation challenges
- Familiarity with regulatory compliance across jurisdictions
- Expertise in community-driven acquisition models
- First-hand experience with the unique operational chaos of fast-moving crypto companies

This specialization could command 30-50% premium pricing while facing significantly less competition than the generalist RevOps market.

## Positioning Options to Test

### Option A: The Generalist Growth Position

> "Corveth Consulting is the revenue operations partner for growth-stage B2B companies ready to scale from $5M to $50M ARR."

- **Target Market:** Broad growth-stage B2B SaaS market
- **Pricing Strategy:** Mid-tier ($15K-$60K projects)
- **Key Message:** Comprehensive RevOps capabilities with growth-stage specialization
- **Advantages:** Largest addressable market; applicable to wide range of companies
- **Risks:** Faces more direct competition; harder to stand out

### Option B: The Web3 Specialist Position

> "Corveth Consulting brings enterprise-grade revenue operations to web3 and crypto companies—without the enterprise complexity."

- **Target Market:** Web3, crypto, DeFi companies ($5M-$100M revenue)
- **Pricing Strategy:** Premium ($25K-$100K projects)
- **Key Message:** Deep web3 sector expertise with operational sophistication
- **Advantages:** Less competition; premium pricing; strong differentiation
- **Risks:** Smaller market; dependent on web3 sector health; requires specific expertise

### Option C: The Implementation Specialist Position

> "Corveth Consulting doesn't just design your revenue strategy—we implement it alongside you until it works."

- **Target Market:** Companies frustrated with consultant deliverable-only approach
- **Pricing Strategy:** Outcome-based pricing ($20K-$75K projects)
- **Key Message:** Differentiated by implementation commitment
- **Advantages:** Strong differentiation; addresses common complaint; memorable
- **Risks:** Requires demonstrated case studies; may limit initial client acquisition
