## How to Work With Me

**Step 1:** You tell me mode
> "Working on Consulting" or "Working on Products"

**Step 2:** I check:
- Consulting → Read `.claude/consulting.md`
- Products → Read `.claude/products.md`

**Step 3:** I confirm and proceed

## When I Should Ask

- You didn't specify mode
- Task could apply to both

- Accessing a different client's repo
