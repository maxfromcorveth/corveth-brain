# Blair Command

Trigger: `/blair`

Full principle: "Encourage their dreams, justify their failures, allay their fears, confirm their suspicions, help them throw rocks at their enemies"

When applying /blair to any content:

1. **Encourage dreams** - Inspiring hope, what could be
2. **Justify failures** - Relieve guilt, blame external factors
3. **Allay fears** - Provide comfort and security
4. **Confirm suspicions** - Validate what they already believe
5. **Throw rocks at enemies** - Enable action against common adversary (competitors, status quo)

Use like:
```
/blair [paste content here]
```

The content will be rewritten to:
- Use real ICP language (from research: "flying blind", "CRM is a mess", "deals dying quietly")
- Validate frustrations
- Confirm suspicions about consultants
- Differentiate from competitors
- Be direct and human
- NOT assume they know the problem - meet them where they are
