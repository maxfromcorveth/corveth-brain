Meet the Founder

Max Corveth

I came into revenue operations through an unconventional path. I spent years in web3 marketing—building campaigns for NFT and DeFi projects during the 2020 bull run, then specializing in DeFi copywriting.

What I learned there changed how I think about growth: in crypto, you either figure out sustainable operations fast or you die. There were no corporate playbooks for decentralized finance—we had to build everything from scratch.

Now I apply that scrappy, operational mindset to traditional B2B SaaS. I've been trading actively across crypto, forex, and US equities for years, which gives me a unique perspective on revenue mechanics and financial operations.

I started Corveth to help growth-stage companies build the operational foundation I wish I had when I was scaling teams.
