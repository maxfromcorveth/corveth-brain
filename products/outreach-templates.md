# LeadForge Outreach Templates

## Cold Outreach (Initial Contact)

### Template A (Congrats + Value)
```
Hey {{first_name}},

Congrats on the {{round_type}}! 🎉 Saw you raised {{amount}} — exciting times.

I help fintech founders like yourself generate qualified leads at scale. Instead of spending hours on research, we identify your ideal customers, verify their intent signals, and include personalized outreach drafts.

Work with another {{industry}} founder recently who saw {{result}} in {{timeframe}}.

Happy to share a quick 10-min demo if you're interested.

Best,
Hari
```

### Template B (Problem-Aware)
```
{{first_name}},

Noticed you're scaling {{company}} — user acquisition must be keeping you busy.

We help post-seed fintechs build consistent lead flow without the SDR overhead. Our AI researches prospects, scores intent, and drafts personalized outreach.

Recently helped a {{similar_company}} go from {{from}} to {{to}} leads/month.

Curious — what's your current biggest bottleneck: traffic or conversion?

Best,
Hari
```

### Template C (Short/Direct)
```
{{first_name}},

Quick question — how are you handling lead gen these days?

We work with funded fintechs who need consistent, qualified prospects without the manual grind. Happy to share what's working for others in your space.

5-min chat?

Hari
```

## Follow-Up Sequences

```
If it's not a priority right now, no worries. Just wanted to make sure it was on your radar.

Happy to share some free insights regardless.

Best,
Hari
```

### Follow-Up 2 (7 days after)
```
{{first_name}},

Last ping on this — I know you're heads down building.

If lead gen ever becomes a pain point, we're here. We've helped {{number}} fintech founders scale their outreach.

No pressure.

Hari
```

## Twitter/X DMs (Shorter)

### DM Template
```
{{first_name}} — saw your launch! Congrats 🎉

We help fintech founders generate qualified leads on autopilot. Happy to share what we're seeing work in {{industry}}.

DM me if interested in a quick chat.
```

---

## Personalization Variables

- {{first_name}} — from LinkedIn/Twitter
- {{company}} — company name
- {{round_type}} — Seed, Series A, etc.
- {{amount}} — funding amount
- {{industry}} — fintech, crypto, etc.
- {{role}} — CEO, CTO, etc.
- {{result}} — specific metric (e.g., "3x pipeline")
- {{timeframe}} — e.g., "first 60 days"
