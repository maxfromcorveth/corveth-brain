# Twitter Content Calendar — Week 1

## Strategy (Based on Research)

### Core Principles
- **Hook formulas:** Strong declarative, controversial opinion, specify WHO, curiosity gap, weird insight
- **Best times:** 9AM-12PM EST on weekdays (Tuesday best)
- **Frequency:** 3-5 posts/day for optimal reach
- **Thread structure:** Hook → Body (1 idea/tweet) → CTA

### Daily Schedule
| Slot | Time (EST) | Content Type |
|------|-------------|--------------|
| Morning | 8-9 AM | Thought leadership / Question |
| Midday | 12-1 PM | Thread / Deep value |
| Evening | 5-6 PM | Reflection / CTA |

## Posting Rules (For Future Reference)

1. **Always hook first** — First 3 words must stop the scroll
2. **Specify WHO** — Name your exact audience
3. **One idea per tweet** — No double meanings
4. **End with CTA** — Reply, follow, DM, or save
5. **Best times:** 8-9 AM, 12-1 PM, 5-6 PM EST
6. **Best days:** Tuesday-Thursday
7. **Engage within 1 hour** — Reply to comments quickly
