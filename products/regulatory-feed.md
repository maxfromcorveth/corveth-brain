# Regulatory Intelligence Feed

## Product
Daily automated briefings on regulatory changes for fintech founders.

## The Problem
> "Regulatory challenges remain the biggest barrier" — r/fintech
> "The pace of regulatory change is accelerating" — Multiple sources

Founders can't track all the regulatory changes across:
- SEC
- FinCEN
- State banking regulators
- EU MiCA
- FCA

## The Solution
AI monitors regulatory sources → cross-references with your product category → generates daily briefing

## Pricing

| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| Free | $0 | Generic daily digest (what changed) |
| Pro | $97/mo | Personalized to your product category |

## Value

- **Free**: 5-min daily read on what changed
- **Pro**: Only relevant changes (filter by: payments, lending, crypto, insurance, banking)
- **Enterprise**: What to do about it
