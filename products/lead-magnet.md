## Free Guide: "5 Lead Gen Mistakes Fintech Founders Make"

---

### Mistake #1: Buying Data Instead of Building Relationships
Most founders buy CSV lists. Problem: Everyone has the same data. Differentiation comes from personalized outreach, not better data.

### Mistake #2: Broadcasting Instead of Nurturing
One email blast ≠ strategy. Real lead gen is a conversation, not a broadcast.

### Mistake #3: Chasing Volume Over Quality
1,000 leads at 1% conversion = 10 conversations
100 leads at 20% conversion = 20 conversations

Quality > Quantity.

### Mistake #4: No Scoring System
Not all leads are equal. A funded startup has different intent than a pre-seed idea. Score by: funding stage, team size, recent activity.

### Mistake #5: Outsource Before Validate
Don't hire an SDR until you've validated the offer. Test yourself first.
