# Research Service Prototype

## Product: Fintech Brief

**Niche:** Daily regulatory intelligence for fintech founders

## What It Is
- Daily 5-minute brief on fintech regulatory changes
- Delivered as PDF via email
- AI researched + written

## Target Customer
- Fintech founders (seed-series A)
- Problem: Can't track regulatory changes themselves
- Willing to pay: $19-49/month

## Skills Used
1. **search** - Find regulatory news
2. **deep-research** - Analyze impact
3. **ai-writing** - Create brief

## Revenue Model
| Tier | Price | Deliverable |
|------|-------|--------------|
| Free | $0 | Weekly email (100 words) |
| Pro | $19/mo | Daily PDF (500 words) |
| Premium | $49/mo | Daily + monthly deep-dive report |

## Execution
1. Set up daily scrape of SEC, FinCEN, regulatory news
2. Use AI to summarize + write brief
3. Email to subscribers
4. Convert free → paid
