## Overview

### Purpose

This SOP establishes a systematic approach for managing inbound LinkedIn connection requests received by Holdex's CEO, Vadim Zolotokrylin. The system ensures every legitimate lead is properly qualified, responded to, and routed through the appropriate pipeline while avoiding time wasted on low-quality or irrelevant connections.

### Scope

This SOP covers:

- Evaluation and classification of incoming connection requests
- Response protocols for each lead tier
- Attio CRM integration for pipeline management
- Daily time allocation (15-30 minutes)

### Target Audience

The primary audience for Holdex's LinkedIn outreach includes:

- **Institutional DeFi Builders** — Teams building decentralized finance infrastructure
- **Tokenized RWA Projects** — Real-world asset tokenization initiatives
- **Blockchain Protocols** — Layer 1/Layer 2 protocols and infrastructure providers
- **Web3 Founders** — Entrepreneurs building in the Web3 space
- **Node Operators** — Infrastructure service providers
- **DAO Operators** — Decentralized autonomous organization teams

### Why This System Matters

Without a structured approach, Holdex risks:

- Missing high-quality leads due to ad-hoc response times
- Wasting time on low-quality or irrelevant connections
- Inconsistent follow-up leading to lost opportunities

## Lead Classification Framework

Every inbound connection request must be classified within 24 hours of receipt. Use the framework below to categorize leads and determine the appropriate response path.

### Classification Criteria

| Tier | Name | Description | Response Time | Action |
|------|------|-------------|---------------|--------|
| **Tier 1** | 🔥 Hot | Active project matching Holdex's portfolio (DeFi, RWA, infrastructure) with clear co-building potential | Same day | Personal outreach, book meeting |
| **Tier 2** | ⭐ Warm | Relevant Web3 project in early stages, potential future alignment | Within 3 days | Nurture sequence |
| **Tier 3** | 📌 Nurture | Service providers, investors, or tangential connections | Within 7 days | Periodic touchpoints |
| **Tier 4** | ❌ Pass | Job seekers, unrelated industries, spam | No response needed | Ignore or polite decline |

### Tier Definitions with Examples

#### Tier 1 — Hot (Priority: Immediate Action)

Leads that match ALL of the following:

- **Active Project**: Has a live product, testnet, or clear MVP
- **Relevant Sector**: DeFi protocol, RWA platform, blockchain infrastructure, or Web3 application
- **Stage Fit**: Pre-seed to Series A (seeking technical co-building partner)
- **Founder Quality**: Technical background, prior entrepreneurial experience
- **Timing Indicator**: Actively seeking partnerships, funding, or technical support

**Examples:**

- "Founder building a lending protocol for institutional DeFi"
- "Team tokenizing real-world assets (real estate, commodities)"
- "Protocol developing cross-chain infrastructure"

**Response Priority:** Personal message within 24 hours. Aim to book a discovery call within 7 days.

---

#### Tier 2 — Warm (Priority: Nurture)

Leads that match SOME of the following:

- **Relevant Sector**: Web3 but not core Holdex focus area
- **Early Stage**: Idea phase or pre-team
- **Geographic Focus**: Regions outside Holdex's current priority
- **Timing**: Not actively seeking partnership, exploring options

**Examples:**

- "Founder with Web3 idea, pre-MVP"
- "Crypto hedge fund exploring DeFi strategies"
- "Developer interested in blockchain consulting"

**Response Priority:** Personalized connection acceptance with nurture sequence. Quarterly touchpoints.

---

#### Tier 3 — Nurture (Priority: Low)

Leads that are valuable but not immediate:

- **Service Providers**: Agencies, consultants, marketing firms
- **Investors**: VCs, angels, family offices interested in Web3
- **Ecosystem Players**: Media, KOLs, event organizers
- **Corporate**: Large companies exploring Web3

**Examples:**

- "Web3 marketing agency"
- "Crypto-native VC firm"
- "Blockchain event organizer"

**Response Priority:** Connection accepted, added to appropriate nurture track. 90-day touchpoints.

---

#### Tier 4 — Pass (Priority: Ignore)

Leads that should be declined or ignored:

- **Job Seekers**: Looking for employment, not partnership
- **Unrelated**: Non-Web3 industries
- **Spam**: Generic connection requests with no personalization
- **Competitors**: Direct competitors to Holdex

**Examples:**

- "Senior DeFi developer looking for job"
- "Real estate agent"
- "Generic 'I'd like to connect' message"

**Response Priority:** Polite decline or ignore. No CRM entry required (optional: log as "Declined" for tracking).

## Daily Workflow

### Morning Routine (15 minutes)

**Goal**: Review new connection requests and prioritize hot leads

1. **LinkedIn Notifications Check**
   - Open LinkedIn on desktop (not mobile for better review)
   - Navigate to "Invitations" section
   - Review each pending request

2. **Classification**
   - Apply the Lead Classification Framework to each request
   - Note: Spend max 2 minutes per request

3. **Tier 1 Actions**
   - If Tier 1 (Hot) identified → Flag for immediate follow-up
   - Draft personal response using Tier 1 template

4. **Log to Attio**
   - Create new lead records for Tier 1 and Tier 2
   - Add appropriate tags (Tier, Source)

### Mid-Day Quick Check (Optional, 5-10 minutes)

**Goal**: Follow up on pending conversations

1. Check for responses to previous messages
2. Follow up on Tier 1 leads with no response (Day 3 and Day 7)
3. Review any meeting requests

### Weekly Review (30 minutes, recommended Monday)

**Goal**: Pipeline health check

1. **Attio Pipeline Review**
   - Review all leads in "Qualified" stage
   - Move leads through stages or mark as won/lost
   - Check for stale leads (no activity in 14+ days)

2. **Metrics Review**
   - Connection requests received this week
   - Tier distribution
   - Meetings booked
   - Response rate

3. **Template Optimization**
   - Note which templates perform best
   - Adjust templates based on response rates

## Attio CRM Integration

### Setup Overview

Holdex already has Attio configured. This section outlines how to integrate the LinkedIn lead management system into the existing setup.

### Required Custom Fields

Create the following custom fields in Attio for the "People" or "Contacts" object:

| Field Name | Type | Options | Description |
|------------|------|---------|-------------|
| **Lead Tier** | Single Select | Hot, Warm, Nurture, Pass | Classification based on SOP framework |
| **Lead Source** | Single Select | LinkedIn, Referral, Event, Outbound, Other | How the lead was acquired |
| **LinkedIn Profile URL** | URL | — | Direct link to LinkedIn profile |
| **Project Name** | Text | — | Name of the lead's project/company |
| **Project Stage** | Single Select | Idea, MVP, Live, Scaling | Current stage of the project |
| **Project Sector** | Multi-select | DeFi, RWA, Infrastructure, L1/L2, DAO, Tools, Other | Web3 sector focus |
| **Last Contact Date** | Date | — | Most recent outreach date |
| **Next Action** | Text | — | What needs to happen next |
| **Next Action Date** | Date | — | When the next action should occur |

### Pipeline Stages

Configure a pipeline (or use existing pipeline) with these stages:

| Stage | Description | Action Items |
|-------|-------------|---------------|
| **New** | Just classified, needs initial outreach | Send Tier-appropriate message |
| **Responded** | Lead has responded, needs qualification | Qualify and determine next step |
| **Qualified** | Confirmed fit, needs meeting | Book discovery call |
| **Meeting Booked** | Call scheduled | Prepare for meeting |
| **Meeting Completed** | Had initial call | Document outcome, determine next steps |
| **Opportunity** | Valid pipeline opportunity | Move to sales process |
| **Closed Won** | Successfully partnered | Celebrate, move to customer success |
| **Closed Lost** | No fit or no response | Archive with reason |

### Recommended Automation Rules

Since Vadim prefers manual over automation (due to LinkedIn ban concerns), keep automations minimal. However, the following low-risk automations are recommended:

| Trigger | Action | Risk Level |
|---------|--------|------------|
| Lead created with Tier = Hot | Create task: "Priority outreach" | Low |
| No response in 7 days (Tier 1) | Create task: "Follow-up Day 7" | Low |
| No response in 14 days (Tier 2) | Create task: "Follow-up or archive" | Low |

### Attio Workflow for LinkedIn Leads

1. **New Connection Request**
   - Review request on LinkedIn
   - Apply classification
   - Manually create Attio record (no auto-sync to avoid ban risk)

2. **Log Activity**
   - After sending message → Log as "Note" in Attio
   - After receiving response → Update status, log as "Note"

3. **Stage Progression**
   - Move lead through stages based on outcomes
   - Always include a note explaining the reason for the move

4. **Weekly Review**
   - Export or view pipeline
   - Identify stale leads
   - Plan upcoming week's focus

## Pipeline Stages

### Detailed Stage Definitions

#### 1. New

**Definition**: Lead has been classified and logged in Attio, but no outreach has been sent yet.

**Entry Criteria**:

- Lead classification complete (Tier assigned)
- Basic information captured in Attio
- Initial message drafted

**Exit Criteria**:

- Initial message sent
- Next action date set

**Typical Time in Stage**: < 24 hours

---

#### 2. Responded

**Definition**: Lead has responded to initial outreach.

**Entry Criteria**:

- Lead replied to LinkedIn message
- Response indicates some level of interest

**Exit Criteria**:

- Lead qualified (move to Qualified)
- Lead not qualified (move to Nurture or Closed Lost)
- No further response (set follow-up task)

**Typical Time in Stage**: 3-7 days

---

#### 3. Qualified

**Definition**: Lead has confirmed interest and fit for Holdex's services.

**Entry Criteria**:

- Lead responded positively
- Project aligns with Holdex focus
- Founder open to initial conversation
- Timing is appropriate

**Exit Criteria**:

- Meeting booked (move to Meeting Booked)
- Lead needs more nurturing (move to Nurture)
- No fit (move to Closed Lost)

**Typical Time in Stage**: 1-3 days

---

#### 4. Meeting Booked

**Definition**: A discovery call or meeting has been scheduled.

**Entry Criteria**:

- Meeting time confirmed
- Calendar invite sent or agreed

**Exit Criteria**:

- Meeting occurred (move to Meeting Completed)
- Meeting cancelled/no-show (return to Responded or Closed Lost)

**Typical Time in Stage**: 1-14 days

---

#### 5. Meeting Completed

**Definition**: Initial discovery call has taken place.

**Entry Criteria**:

- Meeting held
- Notes documented in Attio
- Next steps determined

**Exit Criteria**:

- Valid opportunity identified (move to Opportunity)
- No immediate fit but future potential (move to Nurture)
- No fit (move to Closed Lost)

**Typical Time in Stage**: 1-7 days post-meeting

---

#### 6. Opportunity

**Definition**: Valid business opportunity with clear next steps toward partnership.

**Entry Criteria**:

- Clear project scope identified
- Holdex can provide required services
- Founder wants to proceed with deeper discussion

**Exit Criteria**:

- Partnership formed (move to Closed Won)
- Deal stalled (move to Nurture)
- Deal lost (move to Closed Lost)

**Typical Time in Stage**: 2-8 weeks

---

#### 7. Closed Won

**Definition**: Successfully formed a partnership or engagement.

**Entry Criteria**:

- Formal engagement agreed
- Contract signed or verbal commitment

**Exit Criteria**: None (terminal state for this pipeline)

---

#### 8. Closed Lost

**Definition**: No partnership will be formed.

**Entry Criteria**:

- Lead explicitly declined
- No response after 3+ follow-ups
- Clear misalignment identified

**Exit Criteria**: None (terminal state)

**Required**: Always include "Loss Reason" note

## Best Practices

### Do's

1. **Respond quickly to Tier 1 leads** — Speed matters in Web3
2. **Personalize every message** — Reference their specific project
3. **Log everything in Attio** — If it's not logged, it didn't happen
4. **Follow the classification framework** — Consistency is key
5. **Review weekly** — Pipeline health requires regular attention
6. **Escalate when appropriate** — Don't sit on urgent items

### Don'ts

1. **Don't accept every connection** — Be selective, protect Vadim's time
2. **Don't use auto-reply tools** — LinkedIn bans are a real risk
3. **Don't skip the classification step** — Even for "obvious" leads
4. **Don't forget to set next action dates** — Leads will go stale
5. **Don't ignore Tier 3** — They may become relevant later
6. **Don't over-automate** — Manual approach is preferred here
7. **Don't skip the weekly review** — The system only works if maintained

### LinkedIn Account Protection

Since Vadim prefers manual over automation, these guidelines help protect the account:

1. **No automated connection requests** — Send manually
2. **No automated follow-ups** — Reminders in Attio, but send manually
3. **No third-party tools with LinkedIn access** — Avoid anything requiring LinkedIn OAuth
4. **Normal human patterns** — Don't send 100 messages in an hour
5. **Quality over quantity** — 10 personalized messages beat 50 generic ones
