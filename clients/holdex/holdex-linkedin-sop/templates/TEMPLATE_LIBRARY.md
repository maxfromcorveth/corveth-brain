## Tier 1 — Hot Lead Templates

Use these when you identify a Tier 1 lead: active project, relevant sector, clear entrepreneurial intent.

### Initial Outreach — Project Focus

**Best for**: Founders with live products or MVPs in DeFi, RWA, or Infrastructure

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for the connection! I saw your work on [Project Name] — impressive what you're building in the [Sector] space.

At Holdex, we partner with founders from concept through execution as a technical co-building studio. We've helped teams like [Relevant Example 1] and [Relevant Example 2] go from idea to launched product.

Your project sounds aligned with what we do. Would you be open to a 20-min chat this week to explore if there's a fit? No pitch — just a conversation to understand what you're working on and where you might need support.

Let me know what works for you.

Best,
Vadim
Co-Founder & CEO, Holdex
```

## Tier 4 — Decline Templates

Use these only when you want to politely decline (most Tier 4 leads can simply be ignored).

### Polite Decline — Wrong Focus

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for your interest in connecting. At this time, I'm focusing on building with technical founders on active projects in [DeFi/RWA/Infrastructure], so I'll have to pass.

I wish you the best with [Company/Project].

Best,
Vadim
```

---

### Polite Decline — Too Early

```
Hi [Name],

Thanks for reaching out. Your project sounds interesting, but we're typically further along in our partnerships — working with teams that have at least an MVP.

As you progress, I'd be happy to reconnect. Best of luck!

Best,
Vadim
```

## Notes on Customization

### Variables to Always Customize

| Variable | Example | Why It Matters |
|----------|---------|----------------|
| `[Name]` | Vadim, Sarah | Personalization |
| `[Project Name]` | Clearpool, Aave | Shows you did research |
| `[Sector]` | DeFi, RWA, Infrastructure | Shows sector understanding |
| `[Problem They Solve]` | Cross-chain lending, Real estate tokenization | Shows problem understanding |
| `[Referral Name]` | Alex, someone you both know | Social proof |

### Tips for Higher Response Rates

1. **Reference something specific** — Mention their latest post, a recent news about their project, or something unique about their approach

2. **Keep it short** — Shorter messages (under 100 words) get higher response rates

3. **Offer value first** — Mention what you can help with, not just what you want

4. **Make it easy to say no** — Give them an easy out; it reduces pressure and increases response rates

5. **Match their energy** — If they're formal, be formal. If they're casual, be casual

### Template Rotation

To avoid messages looking generic, rotate between different templates. Track which templates perform best and iterate.
