# Holdex Cold Outreach SOP

## Daily Workflow (30-60 min)

### Before You Start
1. Open the sheet: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/16vNgb579V8ufNBYsO3sk5dyvKf2w_QGYC-FxKGZ5nBk
2. Go to "Cold" tab
3. Filter by Stage = "New"

---

### Sending Connection Requests (Morning - 20 min)

1. Pick a lead from the top (highest priority first)
2. Click LinkedIn URL (column G) → opens their profile
3. Click "Connect"
4. Copy message from **Column J (First Message)** → paste → send
5. In sheet: 
   - Add today's date in **Column M (Sent Date)**
   - Change **Column N (Stage)** to "Reached"

---

### Follow-Up Schedule

| When | Action |
|------|--------|
| Day 3-5 | Filter Stage = "Reached" → check Sent Date → if 3+ days passed, copy from **Column K** → send → note date |
| Day 10 | No response? Copy from **Column L (Breakaway)** → send → change Stage to "Stale" |
| Day 14 | Move on to next lead |

---

### Tracking

- **Stage meanings:**
  - New = not contacted
  - Reached = connection request sent
  - Replied = they responded
  - Meeting = call booked
  - Stale = no response after breakaway

- **Response Notes (Column O):** Write what they said

---

### Weekly Review (15 min)

1. Check Stage = "Replied" — prioritize these
2. Note what messages got responses
3. Update priorities if needed

---

## Tips

- Start with highest priority leads (top of list)
- Keep messages short — they're busy
- If they reply, respond naturally — don't just paste
- Quality > quantity — 10 good messages beats 50 bad ones
